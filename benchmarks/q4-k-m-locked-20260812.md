## Historical repeatability

Two earlier locked-clock runs from a dirty worktree used the same input contract. Their decode medians were 15.110 and 15.105 token/s; the clean-commit median is 15.098 token/s. TTFT medians were 113, 112 and 112 ms respectively. This supports short-run protocol repeatability, but the two dirty runs are not independent reproducibility baselines and none of the three is a long-run stability test.

## Claim boundary

This result supports one locked-clock Q4 text baseline under the exact contract above. It does not establish Q4 versus Q8 gains, VLM image latency or accuracy, RAG quality, concurrent throughput, long-run stability, wall power, or a production SLA. Those claims remain pending separate evidence.
