## Technical Specifications

The hydraulic pump model is BX-9.
Its nominal outlet pressure is 18 MPa.
The permitted fluid temperature range is 15 to 65 degrees Celsius.

## Cavitation Warning

A gravel-like rattling sound with unstable outlet pressure indicates possible
cavitation. Inspect the inlet strainer, suction hose, and reservoir level.

## Alarm E42

On the BX-9, alarm E42 means that motor winding temperature exceeded the safe
limit. Stop the pump and inspect cooling airflow before restarting.

## Maintenance Schedule

Inspect the inlet strainer every 250 operating hours and replace it if damaged.
