# CT-4 Conveyor Controller Manual

Document ID: CT4-MANUAL-001
Revision: 1
Language: en+zh
Classification: synthetic-test-data

## Belt Tracking

The CT-4 reports tracking warning T17 when the belt moves beyond the alignment
sensor limit. Check roller alignment and remove material buildup.

## Emergency Stop Reset

After the guarded area is clear, rotate the emergency-stop button clockwise,
then press the blue reset key for 3 seconds.

## Chinese Operator Note

输送带跑偏代码 T17 表示皮带超出对中传感器范围。检查滚筒对中并清除积料。

## Maintenance Schedule

Inspect belt tension and alignment every 300 operating hours.
