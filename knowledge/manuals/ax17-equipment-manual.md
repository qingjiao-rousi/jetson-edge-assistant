# AX-17 Industrial Compressor Manual

Document ID: AX17-MANUAL-001
Revision: 1
Language: en
Classification: synthetic-test-data

## Technical Specifications

The compressor model is AX-17.
Its nominal operating pressure is 620 kPa.
The rated shaft torque is 42 Nm.

## Alarm E42

Alarm E42 means that cooling loop flow is below the required threshold.
Check coolant level, pump operation, and visible leakage before resetting.

## Maintenance Schedule

Perform the scheduled inspection every 500 operating hours.

## Reset Procedure

After correcting the cooling-flow condition, hold the reset control for
7 seconds. Do not reset the alarm before correcting the underlying condition.
